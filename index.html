How to Play

1

Download the Client

Get the GTPS client from our official download page and install it on your device.

2

Create an Account

Launch the game and register a new GrowID. Make sure to remember your password.

3

Connect to Server

Enter the server IP and port (provided in our Discord) and log in with your GrowID.

4

Start Playing!

Explore worlds, collect gems, trade items, and join events. Have fun!

Tips

  • • Join the official Discord for announcements and support.
  • • Use /help in-game to see all available commands.
  • • Daily rewards are given at midnight server time.
